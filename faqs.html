Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions

Q: What is Dream Shopping Shop?
A: Dream Shopping Shop is a curated collection hub for gadgets, tech, lifestyle, and fashion essentials.
Q: How do I place an order?
A: Click on the product, and you'll be redirected to the seller's page to complete your purchase.
Q: What is the return policy?
A: Returns and exchanges are handled by the seller. Check their policy for details.
Q: Is my payment secure?
A: Yes, payments are processed securely through the seller's website.
Q: Do you offer cash on delivery?
A: COD is available in select areas. Check during checkout.
Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Shipping times vary by product and location. Check estimates on product pages.
Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes, tracking details are provided via email/SMS or on seller's website Track Your Order.
Q: Are products genuine?
A: Yes, We review product photos, customer feedback, material specs, and price points. If a product meets our standards for comfort, quality, and design, it makes it to our Precious Collection 2026. We partner with trusted sellers and brands.
